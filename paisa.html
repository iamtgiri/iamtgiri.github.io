Everything a money tracker needs — nothing it doesn't.

Every feature below runs locally on the device. There's no backend to reach for, so nothing here depends on a server staying online.

↕

Transactions & accounts

Multiple accounts, transfers, tags, favorites, search, and filters — with balances that stay accurate through every edit, undo, and duplicate.

◔

Budgets, goals & recurring bills

Per-category budgets with alerts, savings goals with progress tracking, and recurring bills with an exact due-date — rent on the 5th, SIP on the 3rd.

⌗

Optional auto-capture

Opt-in, off by default: locally reads bank notifications and turns them into a Pending Transaction you review and confirm — nothing posts automatically.

◈

Financial Health score

A 0–100 score built from your Needs/Wants/Savings split, with an outlier-resistant month-end spending projection and a clear explanation of the score.

▥

Analytics that go deep

Category breakdowns, 6-month trends, a daily spending heatmap, a local net-worth chart, weekday patterns, and your biggest expenses ranked.

⇄

Safe backup & export

Full JSON backup and CSV export. Restores are validated before anything is touched, and a safety copy is made automatically before every import.

◷

Reports you can share

A structured monthly report — income, spending, budgets, top expenses — copied or shared as plain text, only when you choose to.

⏰

Local notifications

Budget alerts, recurring due reminders, and daily/weekly/monthly spending summaries — all generated on-device, never through a push service.

◐

PIN lock & themes

An optional PIN for casual privacy, dark and light themes, and configurable currency formatting.

✦

Built with "vibe coding"

Paisa is a personal project developed with heavy help from AI coding tools rather than a professional engineering team. Features, fixes, and even this page were written and iterated on together with an AI pair-programmer.

It's not being oversold as a polished commercial product — it's a real, working app that's actively improved, with the usual rough edges of an independently built side project. Issues and feedback on GitHub are welcome.

⌂

Private by default, not by promise.

Paisa stores everything on your device using a local database and local preference files. There's no account, no server, and no cloud sync to opt out of — because it was never built.

  • The optional notification-based auto-capture is off by default, requires your explicit Android permission, and never posts a transaction without your confirmation.
  • The optional PIN is a casual-privacy convenience, not encryption — it doesn't replace your device's own security.
  • Exported backups can contain sensitive data; store them somewhere only you control.
