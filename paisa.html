The everyday money picture.

Keep the details together, then get back to your day. Paisa brings the essentials of personal finance into one simple app.

↕

Track transactions

Record income, expenses, and transfers across your accounts.

◷

Plan ahead

Set budgets, savings goals, and recurring transactions.

▥

See your patterns

Review spending by category, account, month, or date range.

◴

Know your rhythm

Get optional daily, weekly, and monthly spending reports with period comparisons.

⇄

Keep your own copy

Import and export your local data for backup or migration.

Private by design.

Your financial records stay on your device. Paisa does not need an account, a server, or cloud sync. You decide when and where to store an exported backup.
